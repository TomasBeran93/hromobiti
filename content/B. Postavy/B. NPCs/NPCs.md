## Azam
A merchant, caravan master, and member of the Lords’ Alliance. Azam travels with the characters throughout the adventure.

## Chandra Stol
A quiet female half elf follower of Mielikki, Chandra pays the characters for arriving with the statue for the Shrine of Axes.

## Raggnar Redtooth
A heavyset human man native to Parnast, owner of Golden Tankard (Zlatý Korbel).
