1. **What did your character have for breakfast?**
    - This has always, and likely will always, be the first RP warmup question I ask in a campaign. It goes back to my filmmaking days and needing people to talk so we can balance the microphone levels because it’s such an easy question to think and talk about.
2. **Is your character an early bird or a night owl?**
3. **What is your favorite character’s comfort food?**
4. **Do you have a song that you feel embodies your character? Either as a theme, or as a general vibe.**
    - I like to ask this one early in the campaign so I can try to find an instrumental version of everyone’s songs, which I can either play during notable character moments or when they’ve completed their personal quests.
5. **If your character was a food dish, what would they be?**
6. **What was your character’s last dream about?**
    - This is a perfect question for when your last session ended with everyone going to sleep because now the characters get to immediately respond to those dreams. It’s also one of those questions you can reuse multiple times.
7. **What current possession has your character owned the longest?**
8. **What was your character’s favorite toy as a child?**
9. **What is something that would make your character homesick?**
10. **When was the last time your character was sick? What did it feel like? How bad was it?**
11. **What was something your character would have chatted to everyone about at the last camp or dinner that was missed in-game?**
    - The goal of this one is not for plot advancement or retconning particular moments, but quite the opposite. It’s really easy to gloss over the mundane moments in TTRPGs because we all want to push on with the story, but sometimes those mundane moments are what really connect us to our characters. The answer to this could be as simple as the quality of the boiled potatoes at dinner, or as comprehensive as a theory on the meaning of life from the character’s perspective. Just like the dreams question it can be used multiple times.
12. **Does your character have a favorite idiom (or metaphor)?**
13. **Which animal would your character trade places with for a day?**
14. **What was your character’s education like?**
15. **If your character had to give an 18 minute TED talk, what would the subject be?**
16. **What is your character’s favorite part of where they grew up?**
17. **What is your character’s _least_ favorite part of where they grew up?**
18. **What is a _happy_ memory for your character?**
19. **What is a _sad_ memory for your character?**
20. **Does your character have any plans or dreams for retirement from this life?**
21. **Who is somebody your character can always turn to for help?**
22. **What is your character’s greatest fear?**
23. **When was the last time your character felt completely alone? Why?**
24. **What mystery is your character most intrigued by?**
    - Although the world we play in is my own homebrew setting, I invite the players to create their own in-world mysteries to suit their characters; Things comparable to big-foot or the Bermuda Triangle in our world.
25. **What is your character’s most prominent character flaw, and what balances it out?**
26. **Describe your character’s feelings towards the setting’s current government.**
27. **What is a social or political issue your character feels strongly about? Why?**
28. **What is a _con_ of organized religion?**
29. **What is a _pro_ of organized religion?**
30. **What is your character’s answer to the _trolley problem_?**
    - As there are a variety of versions to this moral dilemma floating around, for simplicity’s sake let’s follow this one:  
        _Situation 1:_ There is a trolley hurdling towards 5 people bound on the tracks. The only impact your character can make is to switch the tracks to one where a single person is bound on the tracks. Does your character switch the tracks? Why or why not?  
        _Situation 2:_ There is a trolley hurdling towards 5 people bound on the tracks. Your character can stop the trolley in time only by pushing somebody in front of the tracks. Do they do so? Why or why not?
        
        - This isn’t a ‘right vs wrong’ or ‘gotcha’ scenario. It’s simply about having your players consider their own character’s thought processes in how they balance which lives they save and their method/consequences by doing so.
